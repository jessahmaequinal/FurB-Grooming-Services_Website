Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about shifts, holds, refunds, and AWD support.

💬 Contact Support

Q: How does the shift-based appointment system work?

A: Each day offers three 4-hour grooming shifts: Shift 1 (7:00 AM – 11:00 AM), Shift 2 (12:00 NN – 4:00 PM), and Shift 3 (5:00 PM – 9:00 PM). Each shift has a fixed capacity of exactly 3 pets to ensure calm, unhurried treatment. The periods between 11:00 AM–12:00 NN and 4:00 PM–5:00 PM are dedicated sanitization and staff rest breaks.

Q: How does the 15-minute payment hold work?

A: When you submit a booking, the shift capacity is immediately reserved On Hold for 15 minutes with a live timer. Once your simulated payment (20% down payment or full amount) is confirmed, your booking becomes Confirmed. If the 15-minute countdown runs out, the reserved slot is automatically released to other customers.

Q: What is the cancellation and refund policy?

A: Cancellations made at least 24 hours before your appointment shift start time receive a 100% full simulated refund of amounts paid online. Cancellations made less than 24 hours prior to the shift are non-refundable as the grooming slot was reserved for your pet.

Q: What is the Animals with Disability (AWD) Discount?

A: We proudly offer an inclusive care discount! Pet parents whose pets have physical challenges, blindness, amputation, or mobility difficulties can declare their condition and optionally upload supporting documents to receive an automatic 20% discount on their entire booking total.

Q: Can I reschedule my appointment?

A: Yes! You can reschedule upcoming appointments directly through My Bookings at zero additional fee. The system checks capacity on your desired new shift before releasing your existing slot, keeping your payment and reference code secure.

Terms & Conditions

Service terms, booking policies, and salon standards for pet safety.

1. Appointment Capacity & Pet Welfare

Each shift is strictly restricted to a maximum of 3 pets to guarantee individual care and prevent stress. Pet owners must ensure their pet is reasonably vaccinated and free of severe contagious conditions upon drop-off.

2. Payment Terms

Customers may choose between a 20% down payment or 100% full payment at the time of online reservation. Any remaining balance is payable in cash upon picking up your groomed pet at our shop.

3. Cancellation and Rescheduling Policy

Appointments may be rescheduled to another shift at no penalty provided the new shift has available capacity. Cancellations made at least 24 hours in advance receive a full simulated refund.

Privacy Policy

How customer and pet information is collected, stored, and protected.

1. Information Collected

We collect customer account information including your name, email address, phone number, and birthdate. For grooming preparations, we collect pet details including pet name, species, breed, recorded weight, care notes, pet photos, and optional AWD disability declarations.

2. Use of Information

Information is used exclusively to facilitate shift scheduling, calculate grooming durations and pricing, contact you regarding appointment updates, and prepare personalized grooming care.

3. Storage and Protection

Customer passwords and authentication sessions are secured via industry-standard protocols. Pet photos and AWD supporting documents are stored separately and securely.
