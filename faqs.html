Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Answers to common questions about booking, payments, and shifts.

Q: How does the shift-based appointment system work?

A: Each day offers three 4-hour grooming shifts: Shift 1 (7:00 AM – 11:00 AM), Shift 2 (12:00 NN – 4:00 PM), and Shift 3 (5:00 PM – 9:00 PM). Each shift has a fixed capacity of exactly 3 pets to ensure high-quality, stress-free care. Periods between 11:00 AM–12:00 NN and 4:00 PM–5:00 PM are dedicated breaks.

Q: How does the 15-minute payment hold work?

A: When you confirm your booking review and submit, your slot is placed On Hold for 15 minutes. If simulated payment (20% down or full) is completed, your booking becomes Confirmed. If the 15-minute countdown expires, the hold is automatically cancelled and the slot is made available to other customers.

Q: What is the cancellation and refund policy?

A: If you cancel at least 24 hours before your scheduled appointment shift start time, you receive a full simulated refund of amounts paid online. Cancellations made less than 24 hours prior are not eligible for a refund.

Q: What is the Animals with Disability (AWD) Discount?

A: Pet owners with pets experiencing physical disabilities, mobility difficulties, or special needs can declare their condition and optionally upload a supporting document to receive an automatic 20% demonstration discount on their booking total.

Q: Can I reschedule my appointment?

A: Yes! You can reschedule upcoming appointments directly through My Bookings. The system checks capacity on the new shift before releasing the previous slot. Rescheduling retains your original reference number and payment without extra fees.

Terms & Conditions

Appointment rules, payment obligations, and salon policies.

1. Appointment Capacity & Pet Welfare

Each shift is restricted to a maximum of 3 pets to guarantee individual attention and prevent distress. Pet owners must ensure their pet is reasonably vaccinated and free of severe infectious conditions upon drop-off.

2. Payment Terms

Customers may choose between a 20% down payment or 100% full payment at the time of online reservation. Any remaining balance is payable upon picking up your groomed pet at our shop.

3. Cancellation and Rescheduling Policy

Appointments may be rescheduled to another shift at no penalty provided the new shift has available capacity. Cancellations made at least 24 hours in advance receive a full simulated refund.

Privacy Policy

How customer and pet information is collected, stored, and protected.

1. Information Collected

We collect customer account information including your name, email address, phone number, and birthdate. For grooming preparations, we collect pet details including pet name, species, breed, recorded weight, care notes, pet photos, and optional AWD disability declarations.

2. Use of Information

Information is used exclusively to facilitate shift scheduling, calculate grooming durations and pricing, contact you regarding appointment updates, and prepare personalized grooming care.

3. Storage and Protection

Customer passwords and authentication sessions are secured via industry-standard protocols. Pet photos and AWD supporting documents are stored separately and securely.
