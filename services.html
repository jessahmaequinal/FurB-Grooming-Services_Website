Pricing Rules & Booking Guidelines
  • Customers select either one grooming package OR one or more à la carte services (not both in a single session).
  • Organic Shampoo Upgrade is already included in Spa Grooming and will not be charged or counted again.

  • Maximum grooming duration is 240 minutes per pet across all shifts.
