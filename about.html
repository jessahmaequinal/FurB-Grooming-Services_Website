Fur & Bubbles Grooming Services

Est. 2026 · Navotas City

A pet grooming haven built on compassion, professionalism, and a genuine love for every fur baby.

Fur & Bubbles Grooming Services is a dedicated pet care establishment located along Davila St., Navotas City, Philippines. Founded and managed by Paulene Serrano, it grew out of genuine love for animal welfare into a professional pet grooming facility designed to offer compassionate, hygienic, and stress-free care for dogs and cats.

🎯

🎯 Our Mission

To empower pet parents with an effortless and transparent online appointment scheduling experience. We provide upfront pricing, clear shift availability, and professional handling to eliminate long shop queues and anxiety for both pets and owners.

🌟

🌟 Our Vision

To be Navotas City's most trusted and beloved pet grooming haven, recognized for exemplary pet hygiene standards, modern digital convenience, and empathetic care for animals of all sizes and abilities.

Inclusive Care Commitment (Animals with Disability — AWD)
